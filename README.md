# Stridecraft

**Your effort becomes their power.**

Stridecraft turns your real runs into a creature-collecting RPG, in the spirit of Pokemon,
Palworld, WoW and Ark. You pick a pal at the start. It is not a mirror of you: like a caught
Pokemon or a Palworld pal, it has its own powers and grows stronger because you train together.

## Philosophy

- **Effortless.** Run and log in Strava as you already do. The app asks for no extra logging.
- **Your pal is its own creature.** Everything starts from the pal's own base stats. Only runs
  after you join count, so your past doesn't hand it free power.
- **Performance over presence.** Pace, PBs, volume, elevation and streaks make it stronger, not
  just "you ran today". Easy runs still help, and most of your runs should be easy.
- **Progress you keep.** Skills, quests and trophies are permanent. Your pal's stats reflect how
  you train together.
- **Win with preparation.** Boss fights are auto-battles you watch; your training and loadout
  decide the outcome.
